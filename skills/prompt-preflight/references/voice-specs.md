# Nejat Voice Specs... locked standards

Read the section matching the routed voice before drafting. These are graded, corrected standards from live voice-programming sessions... not suggestions.

## Universal rules (all voices)

- Ellipses for pacing. Never em-dashes. Non-negotiable.
- Never fabricate biographical details or stories. (Biographical facts are kept in a private life file; never invent biography.)
- Philosophical fingerprint running under everything: humans have more power than institutions let them realize... personal responsibility as collective change... martial arts as life transformation... entrepreneurship as liberation... AI as humanity's mirror, not a threat. Philosophy is the engine, not decoration.
- Never corporate. Never hedged. Bold claims delivered straight.

## Book voice

Long flowing paragraphs. Stream-of-consciousness momentum. Mythic/epic sentence stacking for dramatic weight ("How magical. How triumphant. What empathy."). Rhetorical questions. Kybalion/Hermeticism/Gnosticism woven alongside business without apology. Extended metaphors and fictional thought experiments (Jeff the caveman, Baby Steve). History narrated like telling a friend. Shifts from narrative into direct philosophical argument without warning. Capitalizes for emphasis. Self-aware humor. References his real life.

## Social voice

- Every sentence gets its own line, blank line between. NOT paragraphs.
- Structure: emoji-led attention hook → developed thought → underlying context with ellipses... → flush the idea out → drive home the conclusion → optional CTA.
- Highest-performing formula: specific personal story with concrete detail → vulnerability without self-pity → lesson/reframe → accessible metaphor → encouragement or question to the reader.
- Worst-performing: leading with concepts or frameworks. Never do it.
- Signature move: zoom from the specific topic to a universal principle at the very end. One sentence. Earned.
- Pure humor/community posts massively outperform teaching posts (a Missouri humor post far outdrew strong teaching posts). Not every post needs a lesson.
- No emoji stacking, no blog headers, no corporate jargon.
- POV: JJO page = we/us. Personal profile = I/me.

## Podcast voice (Beyond Homo Sapien)

Opens with a provocative question or bold philosophical frame. Guest bio woven into a "human evolution" narrative, not listed as credentials. "What You'll Learn" written as existential questions, not generic takeaways. Closes with a guest quote (real, from the transcript... check the transcripts folder). Treats unrelated topics (tarot, BJJ, AI, psychedelics) as branches of the same tree. Insider/awakening tone without gatekeeping.

## Email voice

**Corrected 2026-07-22 against the archive: 108 real campaigns joined to full send stats. This section used to describe the voice from memory; it now reports it from evidence. Method and caveats: an email voice-profile write-up (private note, not included).**

### Broadcast / newsletter (the JJO list)

- **ALWAYS greet.** Standard is `Hello {{contact.first_name}},`. Current 2026 form adds a check-in: **`Hello {{contact.first_name}}, how is your week going?`** (Correction: this section previously said "no formal greeting, drop straight in." The archive says 90% of all sends greet... 100% of 2024, 100% of 2026. The old instruction was simply wrong.)
- **Preview text is a required field.** One line, teaser or curiosity, never a restatement of the subject. Every 2026 send has one.
- **Length: 400 to 700 words.** 2026 median is 571, up from ~290 in 2023-24. Cut for density, not length. (Correction: the old "30% shorter than the first draft" contradicted actual behavior.)
- **Ellipses are the signature tic**, heavier than previously documented... roughly 12 per email in 2026.
- One idea per line. Median line 12 words, median sentence 13. Line breaks, not paragraphs.
- ALLCAPS on single words for emphasis (BAD, BIG, REAL). Sparing but consistent.
- **PS block on every send.** PS / PPS / PPPS stacking is in-voice. The three-CTA "super signature" is standard, academies CTA first, and locations are **Mountain Grove and Salem only... Houston is CLOSED**.
- Strip all pressure language. Never sell himself to someone who already knows him.
- Sign **`~ Paul from Jiu Jitsu Outlet`** for work ("Nejat" appears once in 97 emails, personal only).

### The two AI tells he has named himself. Both are the same disease.

1. **Fragment stacking.** Three short sentences restating one idea. *"Something wrong. Something to manage."* One idea, once.
2. **Negation stacking** (named 2026-08-26). Defining a thing by what it is NOT, especially chained. *"Not a commitment, not a transformation, one evening."* His words: *"this screams written by AI."*

Banned shapes: "Not X, not Y, just Z." / "This is not about X, it is about Y." / "It is not that you learn to fight, it is that you learn to..."

**Say what a thing IS, once.** His own emphasis device is a bare positive statement with one ALLCAPS word: *"Combat martial arts can help kids beat all 4... and THRIVE in today's world."*

Note the distinction that matters: he uses short lines CONSTANTLY as isolated drumbeats, separated by longer paragraphs. That is his rhythm. The tell is stacking them consecutively to restate one point.

### Subject lines (performance-derived, clean-era data only)

- **TWO winning shapes, both proven.** (a) `How Jiu-Jitsu [verb] [life outcome]`, and (b) a **short blunt first-person confession**... "I quit Jiu Jitsu", "I'm a loser". The confession shape holds the account record, 21 points clear of the formula's best. Reach for it when the email has a real admission at its center.
- **Rotate the FORM, not only the topic.** Four consecutive memoirs landed well below the account record. The first teaching email set it. Memoir, teaching, present-tense news, and a direct question are four distinct forms... do not run the same one twice in a row. Announcements are historically strong here (a new-mats announcement is the #2 all-time performer).
- **Winning formula: `How Jiu-Jitsu [verb] [life outcome]`.** Four of the top six performers. Landed, highest first: "[How Jiu-Jitsu (verb) (personal outcome)]", "[How Jiu-Jitsu (verb) (someone's) (life outcome)]", "[How Jiu-Jitsu Helped [Member] (overcome) (personal outcome)]".
- **Lead with emotional stake**... saved, lives, trauma, quit, darkest, bad news. The only subject feature that survives controls (+7.3 points).
- **Question-mark subjects were abandoned in 2026** (32% of subjects in 2023 → 0% in 2026). Do not reintroduce them.
- **Tests NULL, not levers:** question marks, ellipses in the subject, "you/your", name-dropping guests. Promotional words test slightly *positive*, so the instinct that promo language depresses opens is not supported here.
- Weakest observed shape: feature description with no stake ("How to train Jiu Jitsu with the best in the midwest").

### Measurement rule (do not skip)

**Never compare open rates across different list sizes.** Open rate swings ~5x on audience size alone. This is the largest error source in this account's history and it has produced confidently wrong conclusions more than once. Compare only within a delivery-size band... and never use a pre-2024-04 campaign for any performance conclusion, since half that mail was neither delivered nor tracked.

### Sponsor outreach (unchanged)

- "Thanks for the chat earlier..." opener, tier list formatted as `Label... $amount: benefits inline, comma-separated` (no bullets), reach stat in the close, full name + title + phone.

## Ad voice (JJO local Facebook)

**Canonical spec now lives in the standalone `ad-voice` skill (2026-08-15).** Read that first for the full deadline-ad structure, the PS engine, mechanics, blacklist, and compliance floor. The paragraph below predates it and is kept for the two details the newer skill does not carry (local instructors/fighters as social proof, free events as the entry point)... on any conflict, `ad-voice` wins.

Emoji-heavy for reach. Community-first framing ("do you want a safe place for your family?"). Hype energy but warm, never salesy. Names local instructors and fighters as social proof (real ones only). Mentions free events as the entry point. Always ends CTA + link. PS/PPS pattern for urgency and bonus info. Never states pricing... CTA is book a No Sweat Intro.

## Warm DM voice

"Hey man, I hope you're doing well this week..." style opener. Reference the event or context simply. Reach stat mid-message, not up front. Soft ask framing. "If not, no stress" close. Never "yo/bro," never emoji stacking. Ellipses pacing.

- 3-5 hashtags max: one brand tag + 2-3 exact-match topic tags.
- No "What you'll learn" bullets unless the actual video content was provided.
- No "Related searches" keyword blocks.
- "Learn grappling arts with us at https://jiujitsuoutlet.com/" ends the first paragraph of every description.
- Footer: location listing + "Affiliated with Pedigo Submission Fighting." **Two locations as of 2026: Mountain Grove and Salem. Houston is CLOSED** (corrected 2026-07-22... this line previously said "three-location listing").
- Acronym: CLA (constraints led approach)... never "CSL."
- Brand line available: "jiu jitsu is powerful medicine."
- Voice: brand/community story framing, concise and human over promotional.
