# GLB intake gates

## Provenance

- Source filename and immutable archive path
- SHA-256
- Source/tool and generation date
- License or usage status
- Raw-generate or textured derivative

## Structural census

- Scene, node, mesh, primitive, material, image, texture, animation counts
- Indexed/non-indexed triangle estimate
- Welded or separable semantic parts
- UV, normal, tangent, skin, morph, and animation availability

## Visual survival

- HERO-1 silhouette retention
- Governed orbit silhouette retention
- Root/ground contact
- Terminal and thin-feature survival
- Normal continuity and faceting
- UV/material continuity

## World integration risks

- Orb and channel occlusion
- Hit-target obstruction
- Root-socket alignment
- Added draws, texture memory, transparency, and estimated device cost
- Progression or MAD implications

Record rejected assets and why. Do not delete the immutable intake source.
