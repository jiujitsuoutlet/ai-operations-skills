# Ruling taxonomy

- `SELECTED`: chosen candidate or direction; not necessarily merge approval.
- `APPROVED_TARGET`: visual target accepted for a specified build step.
- `APPROVED_SLICE`: named implementation authorized within stated bounds.
- `FIX_SLICE`: current direction retained; listed defects must be corrected on the same line.
- `REJECTED`: target/series cannot advance; preserve evidence and stated cause.
- `HELD`: pending named evidence or founder decision.
- `BANKED`: completed or demonstrated leverage intentionally left unmerged/unspent.
- `PARKED`: future option outside the active campaign.
- `PRE_EXISTING_PASS`: governed property already passing; never manufacture a failure.
- `DEBT`: named unresolved work carried forward.
- `AMENDMENT_REQUIRED`: cannot proceed without a dated MAD change.
- `MERGE_AUTHORIZED`: exact PR/branch may merge with named method and gates.
- `CAMPAIGN_CLOSED`: shipped scope and carried debts recorded; no next campaign implied.

An item may carry multiple non-conflicting classifications, such as `SELECTED` plus `HELD_FOR_DEVICE_GATE`.
