# Founder Ruling Ledger

Record what was ruled, no more and no less.

## Capture

1. Preserve the founder's operative wording and date.
2. Identify subject, branch, commit, evidence reviewed, and the exact gate where the ruling occurred.
3. Classify each item using [references/ruling-taxonomy.md](references/ruling-taxonomy.md).
4. Separate target taste, technical evidence, implementation authority, merge authority, and future debt. Approval of one does not imply another.
5. Write the entry from [assets/ruling-entry.md](assets/ruling-entry.md) into the governed build/campaign log.

## Correction law

Keep the ledger append-only. When a measurement, diagnosis, or classification changes, add a dated correction that names the superseded statement and why. Preserve rejected targets, historical condition-sensitive evidence, and pre-existing passes.

## Authority law

- `approved target` authorizes the specified next procedure only.
- `approved slice` does not authorize merge unless explicitly stated.
- `merge` does not authorize the next slice.
- `held`, `parked`, and `banked` work stays inactive.
- A new member-facing decision, progression meaning, frozen opening, or governed threshold requires the applicable dated MAD amendment.

If language is ambiguous about a consequential action, record the unambiguous portion and stop before the uncertain action. Never infer a broader campaign mandate from praise or acceptance.
